Which edition fits your screen?

  • Phone: Landscape, with your phone turned sideways. It gives the biggest, easiest-to-read text. Portrait works upright too, but you may need to pinch to zoom on the captions.
  • Tablet: Portrait, held upright, reads like a printed comic. Turn it sideways for Landscape.
  • Laptop or desktop: Landscape fills the screen. Use fullscreen and the arrow keys to turn pages.
